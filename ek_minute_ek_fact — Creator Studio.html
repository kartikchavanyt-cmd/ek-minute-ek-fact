04 — Visual style library
Choose a direction to use in your visual prompt. These are original style descriptions for your workflow.

      

        
01. Sacred cinematic
Rich temple atmosphere, warm gold light, detailed textures and reverent framing.

        
02. Ancient manuscript
Hand-painted manuscript feel, aged paper, earthy pigments and ornate borders.

        
03. Epic 3D world
Polished 3D environments, dramatic scale, atmospheric depth and rich materials.

        
04. Folk-art storytelling
Bold shapes, traditional patterns, expressive figures and vibrant flat colours.

        
05. Historical documentary
Grounded locations, natural light, realistic details and documentary framing.

        
06. Mystical atmosphere
Soft haze, glowing particles, moonlit depth and a restrained magical mood.

        
07. Miniature painting
Fine linework, intricate architecture, jewel tones and flat perspective.

        
08. Dramatic silhouette
Strong silhouettes, bold backlight, simple shapes and visual suspense.

        
09. Watercolour tale
Soft pigment blooms, textured paper, gentle edges and warm storytelling.

        
10. Museum artefact
Careful object details, controlled studio light and an archival presentation.

        
11. Celestial epic
Vast skies, cosmic scale, luminous clouds and majestic compositions.

        
12. Clean explainer
Simple visual hierarchy, clear objects, uncluttered educational composition.

      

      
Selecting a style sends its description to the Visual Prompt Builder. It does not generate an image by itself.

02 / WORKFLOW

  

    
01 — Research
Verify the story, distinguish tradition from historical evidence, and note sources before scripting.

    
02 — Write & voice
Build a strong opening, keep the story clear, then generate and review the voiceover.

    
03 — Create visuals
Prepare vertical scenes, maintain visual consistency and check every generated frame.

    
04 — Edit & publish
Add captions, music at a suitable level, a clear ending and an accurate title.
